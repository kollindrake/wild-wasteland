# System Documentation Glossary

| Term                       | Definition                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Action                     | An activity performed by an Actor during its turn, potentially requiring the expenditure of Action Points.                                                                    |
| Action Cost                | The number of Action Points an Actor must expend to perform an Action.                                                                                                        |
| Action Points              | A numerical resource determining how many actions an Actor may perform during a Round of combat. Action Points regenerate at the start of each Round.                         |
| AoE Template               | A physical representation used to determine the area and targets affected by an Area of Effect.                                                                               |
| Area of Effect (AoE)       | A defined region within the game world in which an attack, ability, or other effect can affect multiple targets.                                                              |
| Armor Penetration (AP)     | A numerical value or property that reduces or bypasses an applicable amount of Damage Threshold when resolving damage.                                                        |
| Armor Bonus (AB)           | A numerical value that adds to an Actor's Damage Threshold. AP damage ignores the Armor Bonus.                                                                                |
| Attack Roll                | A dice roll made to determine whether an Actor successfully strikes an intended target.                                                                                       |
| Bonus Action               | An additional or specially designated Action that an Actor may perform when permitted by a game rule or ability. An Actor may take only one Bonus Action per Round.           |
| Combatant                  | Any Actor actively participating in a combat Encounter.                                                                                                                       |
| Status Effect              | A temporary or persistent state that alters an Actor’s capabilities, behavior, or statistics according to specific rules.                                                     |
| Cover                      | An environmental obstruction that provides protection against attacks by partially or completely shielding an Actor.                                                          |
| Damage                     | A numerical measure of harm inflicted upon an Actor, typically reducing Health Points or another applicable resource.                                                         |
| Damage Roll                | A dice roll used to determine the amount of damage inflicted by an attack, weapon, or other harmful effect.                                                                   |
| Damage Type                | A classification of damage based on its source or nature, used to determine how defenses, resistances, or vulnerabilities apply.                                              |
| Duration                   | The length of time an ability, Condition, or other game effect remains active.                                                                                                |
| Encounter                  | A structured interaction involving one or more Actors in which actions, decisions, or conflicts are resolved using the game’s mechanics.                                      |
| Engagement Range           | The distance within which opposing Actors threaten one another in close combat, generally within reach of a melee attack.                                                     |
| Free Action                | An Action that requires no expenditure of Action Points and may be performed when permitted by the applicable rules.                                                          |
| Initiative                 | The mechanic used to determine the order in which Actors act during combat.                                                                                                   |
| Initiative Order           | The sequence in which Combatants take their Turns during a Round, as determined by the Initiative mechanic.                                                                   |
| Opportunity Attack         | An attack made in response to an opposing Actor moving out of Engagement Range, when permitted by the rules.                                                                  |
| Pace                       | A numerical representation of the distance an Actor can move during a its turn.                                                                                               |
| Parry                      | A defensive Rating that establishes the Difficulty Class an attacker must meet or exceed to successfully strike an Actor in melee combat. Derived from the Fighting Skill.    |
| Range                      | The maximum distance to which an attack, weapon, ability, or effect can reach a target.                                                                                       |
| Reaction                   | An Action performed in response to a specific event or trigger, potentially outside an Actor’s own Turn. A Reaction still costs Action Points unless a rule states otherwise. |
| Resistance                 | A defensive property that reduces damage or harmful effects an Actor receives from a specified source or Damage Type.                                                         |
| Round                      | A unit of combat time during which each participating Actor has an opportunity to take a Turn.                                                                                |
| Turn                       | The portion of a Round during which an Actor may perform actions and expend available resources.                                                                              |
| Vulnerability              | A state or property that causes an Actor to suffer increased damage or more severe effects from a specified source or Damage Type.                                            |
| Attribute                  | One of six core Traits representing an Actor’s fundamental physical and mental capabilities: Agility, Brawn, Intellect, Spirit, Perception, and Vigor.                        |
| Background                 | A description of a Player Character’s history, occupation, and experiences before the beginning of the campaign.                                                              |
| Feature                    | A beneficial or detrimental characteristic that modifies an Actor’s abilities, capabilities, or limitations.                                                                  |
| Damage Threshold (DT)      | A numerical representation of an Actor’s protection against incoming physical damage.                                                                                         |
| Derived Statistics         | Numerical values calculated from one or more Traits, representing specific capabilities or limitations of an Actor.                                                           |
| Flaw                       | A detrimental Feature that imposes limitations, penalties, or other disadvantages on an Actor.                                                                                |
| Health Points (HP)         | A numerical representation of how much damage a Actor can sustain before becoming incapacitated or dying.                                                                     |
| Character Level            | A number from 1 to 20 representing a character’s overall advancement. A character’s Character Level determines their Rank. See character-progression.md.                      |
| Perk                       | A beneficial Feature that grants an Actor additional abilities, bonuses, or special capabilities.                                                                             |
| Prerequisite               | A requirement that must be satisfied before an Actor may acquire or use a particular Feature or ability.                                                                      |
| Race                       | The biological or artificial classification of an Actor, determining certain innate characteristics and capabilities.                                                         |
| Radiation Points (RP)      | A measure of an Actor’s accumulated radiation exposure, which may cause progressively harmful effects or death.                                                               |
| Saving Throw               | A defensive Check made to resist, avoid, or reduce the effects of physical, mental, or other harmful threats.                                                                 |
| Skill                      | A Trait representing an Actor’s learned knowledge, training, or practical proficiency in performing specific tasks.                                                           |
| Tagged Skill               | A designation applied to a Skill that allows a Check to automatically succeed when doubles are rolled, except when both dice show one.                                        |
| Trait                      | A characteristic or capability possessed by an Actor. Traits encompass Attributes, Skills, and other rated characteristics.                                                   |
| Trait Rating               | A numerical value assigned to a Trait that determines its effectiveness and any associated modifiers.                                                                         |
| Rank                       | One of five stages of character progression (Drifter, Survivor, Veteran, Renowned, Legend), each covering four consecutive Character Levels and determined by Character Level. See character-progression.md. |
| Advantage                  | A mechanic allowing an Actor to roll an additional die during a Check and retain the highest applicable dice for its result.                                                  |
| Check                      | A Check made using an Attribute to determine whether an Actor succeeds at a task relying primarily on fundamental physical or mental capabilities.                            |
| Critical Failure           | An exceptional outcome of a Check that produces an additional detrimental effect beyond ordinary failure.                                                                     |
| Critical Success           | An exceptional outcome of a Check that produces an additional beneficial effect beyond ordinary success. A natural result of 20 is noted in the scanned draft.                |
| Difficulty Class (DC)      | The target number that a Check must meet or exceed to succeed.                                                                                                                |
| Disadvantage               | A mechanic requiring an Actor to roll an additional die during a Check and retain the lowest applicable dice for its result.                                                  |
| Actor                      | Any independently acting being or construct represented within the game world.                                                                                                |
| Failure                    | The outcome of a Check when its final result falls below the Difficulty Class and no special rule grants success.                                                             |
| Final Result               | The total numerical value of a Check after applying all applicable Modifiers, bonuses, penalties, and other adjustments to the Natural Roll.                                  |
| Fortune Points             | A metagame resource that players may expend to influence dice rolls, activate special abilities, or otherwise affect gameplay.                                                |
| Game Master                | The participant responsible for adjudicating rules, portraying the game world, and controlling Actors not governed by players.                                                |
| Group Check                | A Check involving multiple Actors working toward a shared objective, with the collective outcome determined by their individual results.                                      |
| Help Check                 | A Check made by an Actor attempting to assist another Actor, potentially granting a bonus or other benefit to the assisted Actor’s Check.                                     |
| Modifier                   | A positive or negative numerical value applied to a dice roll, Trait, or Derived Statistic.                                                                                   |
| Natural Roll               | The numerical result shown on the dice before applying Modifiers, bonuses, penalties, or other adjustments.                                                                   |
| Non-Player Character (NPC) | An Actor controlled by the Game Master rather than a player.                                                                                                                  |
| Opposed Check              | A Check in which two Actors roll against one another to determine which succeeds in a contested action.                                                                       |
| Passive Check              | A Check resolved without an active dice roll, using a predetermined value to represent an Actor’s ability to perform or notice something without deliberate effort.           |
| Player Characetr (PC)      | An Actor controlled by a player who makes decisions and performs actions through that character.                                                                              |
| Reroll                     | An additional roll of one or more dice made after an initial roll, as permitted or required by a game mechanic.                                                               |
| Success                    | The outcome of a Check when its final result meets or exceeds the Difficulty Class, or when another rule grants automatic success.                                            |
| Untrained Check            | A Check made when an Actor attempts a task without the necessary training or proficiency, subject to a noted −2 penalty.                                                      |
