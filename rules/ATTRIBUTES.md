# Attributes
---

| Attribute  | Acronym | Defnition                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------- | ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Agility    | AGI     | How quick, coordinated, and precise your character is. Agility affects their reflexes, balance, movement, and ability to perform tasks requiring careful physical control.                                |
| Brawn      | BRN     | How physically strong your character is. Brawn determines their ability to lift heavy objects, overpower opponents, carry equipment, and accomplish tasks through raw strength.                           |
| Intellect  | INT     | How well your character thinks, learns, and solves problems. Intellect determines their ability to understand complex subjects, remember information, reason through situations, and apply knowledge.     |
| Spirit     | SPT     | How strong your character is mentally and emotionally. Spirit determines their courage, determination, self-control, and ability to withstand fear, stress, and psychological pressure.                   |
| Perception | PER     | How aware and observant your character is. Perception governs their ability to notice details, interpret surroundings, recognize patterns, read situations, trust their instincts, and anticipate danger. |
| Vigor      | VIG     | How physically tough and resilient your character is. Vigor determines their stamina, endurance, and ability to withstand injuries, illness, exhaustion, radiation, and harsh environmental conditions.   |
