# Skills
---

| Skill        | Derived Attribute | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------ | ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Athletics    | AGI               | How skilled your character is at performing physical activities that require coordination, balance, speed, and controlled movement. Athletics governs their ability to navigate obstacles, maneuver through difficult environments, and accomplish physically demanding tasks.         |
| Deception    | SPT               | How skilled your character is at misleading, concealing, or manipulating information and appearances. Deception governs their ability to lie convincingly, disguise intentions, create false impressions, and influence others through misdirection.                                   |
| Driving      | AGI               | How skilled your character is at operating and controlling vehicles. Driving governs their ability to maneuver, navigate difficult conditions, maintain control, and handle vehicles in demanding or dangerous situations.                                                             |
| Explosives   | INT               | How skilled your character is at understanding, handling, and utilizing explosive materials and devices. Explosives governs their ability to identify, prepare, deploy, disarm, and assess explosive hazards and their effects.                                                        |
| Fighting     | AGI               | How skilled your character is at engaging in close-quarters combat. Fighting governs their ability to attack, defend, maneuver, and overcome opponents using unarmed techniques, melee weapons, or improvised objects.                                                                 |
| History      | INT               | How knowledgeable your character is about past events, civilizations, cultures, and their lasting influences. History governs their ability to recall historical information, recognize cultural significance, and understand how past events relate to the present.                   |
| Insight      | PER               | How skilled your character is at understanding the behavior, emotions, and intentions of others. Insight governs their ability to interpret social cues, recognize inconsistencies, assess motivations, and anticipate the actions of individuals or groups.                           |
| Intimidation | SPT               | How skilled your character is at influencing others through fear, pressure, or displays of authority. Intimidation governs their ability to issue threats, assert dominance, discourage opposition, and compel cooperation through coercion.                                           |
| Medicine     | INT               | How skilled your character is at understanding and treating injuries, illnesses, and other physical conditions. Medicine governs their ability to diagnose problems, administer treatment, stabilize patients, and apply medical knowledge to preserve or restore health.              |
| Notice       | PER               | How skilled your character is at detecting, identifying, and interpreting details in their surroundings. Notice governs their ability to observe changes, discover hidden information, recognize unusual circumstances, and detect potential threats or opportunities.                 |
| Persuasion   | SPT               | How skilled your character is at influencing others through communication, reasoning, and social interaction. Persuasion governs their ability to negotiate, establish trust, resolve disagreements, inspire cooperation, and convince others to consider their perspective.           |
| Repair       | INT               | How skilled your character is at understanding, maintaining, modifying, and restoring mechanical, electrical, and technological systems. Repair governs their ability to diagnose malfunctions, work with tools and machinery, and develop practical solutions to technical problems.  |
| Riding       | AGI               | How skilled your character is at handling, directing, and controlling animals used for transportation or other purposes. Riding governs their ability to remain mounted, maneuver safely, communicate commands, and maintain control under difficult conditions.                       |
| Science      | INT               | How skilled your character is at understanding and applying scientific principles, theories, and methods. Science governs their ability to analyze information, conduct experiments, investigate phenomena, and solve problems through systematic reasoning and scientific knowledge.  |
| Shooting     | AGI               | How skilled your character is at accurately using ranged weapons. Shooting governs their ability to aim, fire, control, and effectively employ firearms, projectile weapons, and other ranged weaponry under varying conditions.                                                       |
| Stealth      | AGI               | How skilled your character is at avoiding detection and concealing their presence or activities. Stealth governs their ability to move quietly, remain hidden, blend into surroundings, and perform actions without attracting unwanted attention.                                     |
| Survival     | PER               | How skilled your character is at adapting to, navigating, and enduring unfamiliar or hazardous environments. Survival governs their ability to locate resources, track creatures, interpret natural conditions, navigate wilderness, and overcome environmental challenges.            |
| Thievery     | AGI               | How skilled your character is at manipulating objects, bypassing security, and performing actions requiring precise manual dexterity. Thievery governs their ability to pick locks, handle delicate mechanisms, conceal or acquire objects, and circumvent physical security measures. |
